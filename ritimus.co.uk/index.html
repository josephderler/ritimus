The RitimUS Approach

Every Student Is Unique
We first get to know the children and, with our individualized game plan, help them develop by maximizing their abilities.
Assessment Through Games
From the moment children start playing, the games are reported and presented with dedicated, detailed charts inside the app.
Positive Feedback
After measuring the areas where the student excels and needs improvement, the games shown on their screen are determined accordingly.
Successful Results
As a result of this approach, the child fills the gaps in weaker areas while continuing to improve in the areas where they already excel.
